Staff aura options

I play a devil mage, and this is the route I think the staff line should take for mages. Devil mages feel it first. A staff swing gives us no experience and no stats. Casting is how we level.

At level 42 I am still on my second staff aura, Rapid Magic Casting, and I rely on it more than I did at level 22. Staff skill and INT have both climbed a long way in those twenty levels. The spells I level with have not. A higher number on the skill, or on INT, still leaves me casting the same things.

After that aura the staff line is Mana Shield, Repulse, Mage's Contrivance and Blink. They each do a job. None of them is the next thing to look forward to while the hours go into killing monsters. The same casts are the job at 22 and at 42, and nothing further along the staff changes that.

The list below is aimed at that gap. The early options exist so the next ranks of staff skill change those hours. The later options are for fights with other players, kept separate so a levelling aura stays a levelling aura.

These are alternatives. Two auras at once is already how levelling works. If more than two of these could run together, cap active auras at two. Mana Leech stays as it is: a tenth of spell damage as mana, after it was cut down from a half. Mana Shield stays as it is.

The pictures are new drawings in the style of the inventory sprites, so the ideas are easy to tell apart. They are not icons from the client. Prices sit in the same band as the staff auras already sold by Chandalir. The numbers are a starting point to tune.

Past the auras are three spell changes for rifts and dungeons. Mass Heal and Chain Lightning stop being a square on the floor. Mending is a heal that keeps going after the cast.

Worth starting with

Arcane Tempo

Staff 40, with an upgrade at staff 70. Replaces Rapid Magic Casting.

Rank 1 is 20% cast speed. That is the same as Rapid Magic Casting, and it works against monsters and against players.

The upgrade keeps the 20% cast speed, and 8% of damage dealt to a monster comes back as mana. Damage to a player gives none. While the upgrade is up, Mana Leech does not also return mana, so the two do not add.

Cast 3 HP. Rank 1 lasts 60 seconds. The upgrade lasts 2 minutes. 50,000 Barr.

Echo Cast

Staff 120. Replaces Mage's Contrivance.

10% chance, and only when the spell's direct target is a monster. That direct hit fires again at once, at full damage, with no mana and no cast bar. The echo cannot proc itself. It copies damage only, so a stun, a slow or a drain does not double.

Bounces and ground circles are not copied. Chain Lightning does not repeat its jumps. Hellfire does not lay a second circle. If the direct target is a player, the chance is 0.

Cast 3 HP. Lasts 2 minutes. 150,000 Barr. This is the multicast, kept to the one monster you aimed at.

Other levelling jobs

Monster Focus

Staff 80. New. Repulse stays a button. Mana Shield stays.

Spells deal 8% more damage to monsters. A player takes the normal amount. It is a flat bonus, not a proc, and it does not add cast speed or mana.

Cast 3 HP. Lasts 2 minutes. 70,000 Barr.

Second Wind

Staff 100. New.

Killing a monster restores 4% of max HP, at most once every 4 seconds. Killing a player does not heal you. If you and a player have hit each other in the last 10 seconds, a monster kill does not heal you either. That stops eating a weak monster for health during a fight.

Cast 3 HP. Lasts 2 minutes. 100,000 Barr.

First Spark

Staff 25. New. Mana Leech stays.

The first damaging spell that hits a monster, while that monster is still above 80% health, deals 25% extra. Once per monster. A player is never this target. It is an opener for a fresh pull, not a buff that is always on.

Cast 3 HP. Lasts 2 minutes. 15,000 Barr.

Still Cast

Staff 90. New. Useful against monsters and against players.

A hit moves your cast bar 40% less. Nothing about damage, mana or speed changes. It is the one that helps when something is standing on you, whether that something is a monster or a player.

Cast 3 HP. Lasts 2 minutes. 80,000 Barr.

Player fights

Duel Mark

Staff 110. New.

A spell aimed at a player has a 10% chance to finish with no cast time. The mana is still paid. After it happens, it cannot happen again for 8 seconds. A monster does not trigger it. This is a tempo moment in a fight, not a second Echo Cast.

Cast 3 HP. Lasts 2 minutes. 120,000 Barr.

Spell Ward

Staff 130. New. Does not replace Mana Shield.

Spells cast by a player deal 10% less damage to you. Damage from a monster is unchanged. The damage still comes out of health. Mana Shield remains the aura that moves damage into mana.

Cast 3 HP. Lasts 60 seconds. 50,000 Barr.

Last Word

Staff 140. New. Blink at staff 150 stays.

When a player's hit takes you under 30% HP, your next spell within 8 seconds has no cast time. The mana is still paid. It can happen once every 45 seconds. A monster's hit does not arm it.

Cast 3 HP. Lasts 2 minutes. 150,000 Barr.

Crowded fields

Civil Circle

Staff 55. New. A switch, not a damage aura.

While it is up, area spells damage monsters and skip players. Turn it off when an area spell is meant for a fight. Hellfire and Chain Lightning can stay levelling tools in a field where other people are standing.

Cast 3 HP. Lasts 2 minutes. 40,000 Barr.

Healing, and Chain Lightning

In a rift or a dungeon the group does not stand in one square. Mass Heal and Chain Lightning both ask them to. These keep the listed numbers and change the shape. Mending is the extra spell: a heal that carries on after you cast it, so you can turn to the next person while the first is still being healed.

Chain Heal

Mass Heal, cast as a bounce. DaeYoo, Pandemonium, INT 50. The square on the floor goes.

You cast it on yourself, or on a party or guild member. That person is healed for the current Mass Heal amount. Rank 1 is listed at 8. Rank 4 is listed at 14. Both are before INT.

It then jumps to the nearest other party or guild member within 2 steps of the person just healed, and it keeps jumping. Each jump heals the same amount. It will not heal the same person twice, it will not heal a monster, and it will not heal anyone outside the party and the guild.

The chain stops when the next person is more than 2 steps away. There is no headcount. A loose group that can still reach one another gets the whole group. Mana stays 20 at rank 1 and 14 at rank 4. 35,000 Barr, as it is now.

Chain Lightning

The same spell. The square on the floor goes. INT 72.

You aim it at one monster, or at one player. That target takes the current damage. Rank 1 is 35. Rank 4 is 39. Both are before INT and magic attack.

It jumps to the nearest other target within 2 steps, and it keeps jumping. Each jump deals the same damage. Aimed at a monster, the jumps stay on monsters. Aimed at a player, the jumps stay on players. A cast into a pack does not clip a player standing beside it.

The chain stops when the next target is more than 2 steps away. There is no headcount. Mana and cast time stay as they are. Rank 1 is 34 mana and 1.40 seconds. Rank 4 is 28 mana and 1.22 seconds. Echo Cast still does not copy the jumps. Hellfire stays the circle on the ground.

Mending

New. Sits beside Healing Magic and Swift Heal. It replaces neither.

Cast it on yourself, or on one party or guild member. Four heals follow, one every 2 seconds. Each heal is half a Healing Magic of the same rank, so the full 8 seconds equals two Healing Magics. Rank 1 Healing Magic is listed at 10, so each tick is 5.

One Mending on a person at a time. Casting it again replaces the old one. It does not jump. Swift Heal stays the instant heal, a third of their health, on its 8 second cooldown. Rejuvenation stays the heal for the whole party.

Rank 1 costs 6 mana, the cost of two Healing Magics. Higher ranks keep that ratio. It is a cast, not an instant. Devils learn it with Healing Magic. Humans learn it with the White Magician's heals.

One fight, separate from the spells. The solo mage dungeon's final room still brings more melee monsters than casters. That last room should be the magic fight.

Draft, 5 Nov 2026. A suggestion. Not a patch.
